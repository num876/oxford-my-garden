What We Do

Expert landscaping and garden care, tailored to your space.

Garden Tidying & Planting

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide free quotes?
Yes, we offer free, no-obligation quotes for all projects. We prefer to visit your property in person to assess the garden, understand your needs, and provide an accurate price.
Do you take away the garden waste?
Absolutely. For clearances, hedge trimming, and tree cutting, we will clear all the green waste and dispose of it responsibly, leaving your garden completely clean and tidy.
Do you do regular maintenance or just one-off jobs?
We do both. We are happy to tackle massive one-off garden clearances to get things under control, and we also offer regular, scheduled maintenance (mowing, pruning, weeding) to keep it looking perfect.
Are you fully insured?
Yes, Oxford My Garden is fully insured for all landscaping, tree cutting, and maintenance work, giving you complete peace of mind while we work on your property.
